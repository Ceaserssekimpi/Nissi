About RUCKUS Networks

RUCKUS Networks, a leading provider of purpose-driven enterprise networks, offers a range of products and services designed to meet the strict requirements of various enterprise environments. Backed by the corporate resources of CommScope, RUCKUS Networks delivers exceptional network experiences that are trusted by their customers. Their purpose-driven network infrastructure is built and designed to perform in tough and unique industry environments, empowering customers to deliver exceptional experiences for every user.


RUCKUS Networks' Wi-Fi products and AI/ML-driven Wi-Fi assurance have outperformed competitors in independently validated tests, highlighting their advantage in performance. With a single platform for managing multiple access networks, zero-touch provisioning, and intuitive management, RUCKUS Networks ensures simplicity and ease of use. Their interchangeable deployment options also offer easy capacity expansion, ensuring future flexibility and investment protection.


RUCKUS Networks serves various industries including primary and higher education, hospitality, large public venues, and multi-dwelling units. They also offer cutting-edge technologies such as Wi-Fi 7, Wi-Fi 6 and Wi-Fi 6E, network segmentation, and secure onboarding. With a proven track record and a portfolio of satisfied customers, RUCKUS Networks is a reliable choice for purpose-driven enterprise networks
