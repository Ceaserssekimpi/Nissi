WHO WE ARE

Technology Solutions Built Around Your Business.

Nissi Technologies Limited

Your Trusted Partner in ICT Solutions & Services

Nissi Technologies Limited is a Ugandan private ICT company established in 2004, providing reliable computer supplies, technology solutions, system design, maintenance, upgrading, consultancy and technical support services.

Over the years, we have supported private and public institutions, non-governmental organizations, civil society organizations and businesses with practical technology solutions designed to improve productivity, efficiency, communication and service delivery.

Our approach is simple: understand your needs, recommend the right technology, implement it professionally, and remain available when you need support.

“You demand it, we deliver it.”
