Who we are

Technology that works for the way you operate.

Nissi Technologies Limited is a Ugandan private computer supplies and upgrading firm established in 2004. The company provides ICT sales, service, maintenance, system design and consulting to private and public institutions, NGOs and civil society organizations.

Our approach is consultative: understand the business requirement, design the right solution, deploy it professionally, and provide ongoing support.
